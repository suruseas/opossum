- On Apple `container` 1.6.1, a service with `platform: linux/arm64` whose image is local and has an amd64 build and no arm64 one now gets the `OPSM-412` advice from `up`, `up --foreground` and `run`, as it did on earlier versions; 1.6.1 words this refusal differently (`does not support linux/arm64; available: […]`). The advice now says to set `platform: linux/amd64` on the service rather than to add it, since such a service already names a platform.
