> [!WARNING]
> **A service that mounts a part of a named volume no longer starts.**
>
> A named-volume mount with `volume: {subpath: …}` — in a service's own `volumes:` or borrowed through `volumes_from` — is now refused before anything is created, naming the service and the mount. Earlier versions mounted the whole volume in its place without a word, so the service saw other files at that path than it does under docker compose; container 1.4.1 cannot mount a part of a volume. Mount the whole volume and use the path under it, or keep what is under that path in a volume of its own. A bind's or a tmpfs's `subpath`, and one that names the whole volume (`.`), go ahead as before.
