> [!WARNING]
> **A tmpfs mount no longer lets the files in it run.**
>
> A `tmpfs:` mount or a `type: tmpfs` volume is now mounted with docker's defaults `nosuid,nodev,noexec`, so a service that puts a program or script on a tmpfs mount and runs it from there will fail after this upgrade, as it does under docker. Write `exec` on the mount to keep it runnable, as in `/tmp:exec`. The first `up` after upgrading makes containers that have a tmpfs mount again; other containers are left as they are.
